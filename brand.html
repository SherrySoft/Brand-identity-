The visual & verbal identity of Ditervo.

Designed for clarity across software interfaces, AI developer documentation, investor decks, and hardware-inspired product surfaces.

Modular precision in two geometric forms.

The Ditervo mark nests a Signal Teal core inside a Mineral Ink frame—symbolizing structured engineering around applied intelligence.

Light Surface Wordmark

Use on Cool Mineral (#EFF2F1) or white backgrounds with at least 24px clearspace around the lockup.

Dark Surface Wordmark

Use on Mineral Ink (#11161A) or dark presentation slides. Never add drop shadows or neon outer glows.

Icon Mark & Favicon

Use the standalone Ditervo monogram for app icons, social avatars, GitHub organization profiles, and browser favicons.

Download Mark SVG

Usage Rules

  • Always write the brand name in Title Case: Ditervo (or uppercase DITERVO in technical headers).
  • Maintain minimum clearspace equal to the height of the inner teal module.
  • Do not stretch, skew, recolor with gradients, or place over busy photography.
